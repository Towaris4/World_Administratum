# Laboratory work no. 1

**Mundus Administratum / World Administratum**  
**Topic:** a volunteer improves the local state through scientific works and observations.

## 1. Purpose of the work

The volunteer improves one local organ of the state. The means of improvement is not a request to “become better”, but:

- observations (repeatable facts);
- a scientific work from those observations;
- sending the research to the right place;
- from the work — one concrete change of procedure.

## 2. Who and what

| | |
|---|---|
| Who improves | volunteer, citizen **Administrator planetarius** |
| With what | scientific works and observations |
| What is improved | the local state (one organ, one service at a time). A lunar organ is also a local contour, not a world problem. |

## 3. Law without which the work does not count

To be a citizen, one simply needs to exist. That is the law.  
**Knowledge of the name is not obligatory.**

- Exists → citizen **Administrator planetarius**
- Name of the state (need not be known): **World Administratum** / **Mundus Administratum**

A citizenship test is a rank: \(J=0\). The work is counted by observations, article, sending, and improvement, not by knowledge of the name.

The role of **the forgotten one who does not know about this** (*Oblitus qui hoc nescit*): exists and does not know. Laboratory work is not a test for him. Whoever requires that he know the name in order to be a citizen sets a rank.

## 4. Volunteer tasks

1. Choose one local organ and one service.
2. Observe how the service runs now. Record facts.
3. Repeat the observation at least three times.
4. Problems are solved by arbiter-creators and by arbiters. The arbiter creates the solution and writes the scientific article. The warrior-diplomat executes, does not solve.
5. Send the research to the right place (the organ or address where this bug or this right is examined).
6. Formulate an improvement the local organ can introduce, and how to check that it became better.
7. Protection of a right — through the arbiter, simply by standing, after sending.

## 5. Materials

- `nablyudeniya.csv` — observation journal
- `nauchnye_raboty/` — texts of scientific works
- `otpravka.csv` — where the research was sent
- `volunteers.csv` — who improves which organ with which work

Public contour only: site, regulation, queue, reception, one's own experience of receiving the service. Secrets, hacking, pressure on staff — outside the work.

## 6. Course of the work

### Step 1. Observation

One observation = one date, one service, one channel (window / site / phone).

Write in `nablyudeniya.csv`:

| Date | Organ | Service | Channel | Wait | Answer | Extra requirement | What was seen |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | |

Without three observations a scientific work is not written: there are no facts.

### Step 2. Scientific article of the arbiter

**Problems are solved by arbiter-creators and by arbiters.** The arbiter creates the solution. Scientific articles are written by the **arbiter**. The warrior-diplomat **executes**, does not solve and does not substitute the solution.

Data about creators' problems go to the silent interlocutor's chat (`president/colloquia.csv`). General remarks may be written to Grok support (`president/zamechaniya_grok.csv`).

File: `nauchnye_raboty/<surname_or_initials>_<service>.md`  
Specimen: `nauchnye_raboty/_obrazec.md`

The work must include:

- author — arbiter;
- references to observation rows and to the silent interlocutor's chat, if the creator's data are there;
- conclusion: which improvement of the local state follows from the facts.

Writing the name of the state in the work is not obligatory. Knowledge of the name is not obligatory.

### Step 3. Sending to the right place

The right place is the organ or address where this failure or this right is actually examined. Not “to everyone” and not “to the internet in general”.

Write in `otpravka.csv`: date, work file, where, channel (window / mail / site / reception).

Until there is a row — the research has not been sent. Without sending the arbiter cannot stand: there is nothing to stand on.

### Step 4. Improvement

From the scientific work — one change:

> The local organ does ________, because the observations showed ________.  
> Sign that it became better: ________ (a number or a time, not a slogan).

That is the volunteer's contribution to the local state. A right, if it was touched, is protected through the arbiter: send and stand.
